## Categories List

### TPS – Transaction Processing System
- Sales Transactions
- Purchase Orders
- Payment Records

### MIS – Management Information System
- Customer Information
- Inventory Levels
- Supplier Details

### HRIS – Human Resource Information System
- Employee Records
- Job Assignments

### DSS – Decision Support System
- Financial Reports
- Budget Forecasts

### EIS – Executive Information System
- Marketing Data
- Strategic KPIs
